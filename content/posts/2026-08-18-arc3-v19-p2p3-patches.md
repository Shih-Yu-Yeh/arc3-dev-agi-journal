## TL;DR

Added 9 patches: FP8 KV cache, writable bundle copy, reasoning effort cap, noop guard verification, MULTIMODAL_UPSCALE=8. All 9 verified firing in stdout. LB dropped 1.43 to 1.17.

## Context

V17 broke through to 1.43. The hypothesis: more patches = more improvements. V19 stacked 9 patches on top of V17:

- P2 patches: FP8 KV cache, writable bundle copy, reasoning effort cap.
- P3 patches: noop guard verification, MULTIMODAL_UPSCALE=8 (first appearance), Qwen3.8 model, anim bundle.

All 9 patches were verified firing in stdout. The expectation was LB 2.0+.

The result: LB 1.17, -0.26 from V17.

## Technical Choice

The 9 patches and their markers:

1. **FP8 KV cache**: `TAAF pre-setup: ENABLE_FP8_KV=True` + vLLM launched with `--kv-cache-dtype fp8`. Verified.
2. **Writable bundle copy**: `refreshing writable bundle copy` + `BUNDLE_DIR reassigned`. Avoids V11's read-only filesystem error. Verified.
3. **MULTIMODAL_UPSCALE=8**: `Patched MULTIMODAL_UPSCALE to 8 (512x512 vision)`. Verified.
4. **Qwen3.8 model**: `MODEL_SLUG = 'qwen3-8-27b-fp8-hf-snapshot'`. Verified.
5. **Reasoning effort cap**: `solver.chat_template_kwargs set to {'reasoning_effort': 'high'}`. Caps reasoning effort to 'high' (down from xhigh/max). Verified.
6. **Noop guard verification**: `hard_noop_guard = True`. Verified.
7. **Temperature set**: `temperature': 0.0`. Verified.
8. **Context window set**: `ANALYZER_CONTEXT_WINDOW = 32768`. Verified.
9. **vLLM server started**: `vLLM server ready`. Verified.

## Outcome Analysis

LB 1.17, -0.26 from V17's 1.43. All 9 patches fired, but LB regressed.

The attribution problem is severe. 9 patches stacked means 9 possible causes of regression. The most likely candidates:

First, `MULTIMODAL_UPSCALE=8` may have been too aggressive at this stage. The 512x512 vision produces 4x the tokens of 256x256. Combined with the 32k context window, the vision tokens may have crowded out game history. The V24 win later showed that MM_UPSCALE=8 works only when paired with the right bundle (anim, not the V19 bundle).

Second, `reasoning_effort: 'high'` (down from max) may have reduced the solver's ability to reason through complex multi-step games. V17 ran with max reasoning effort and scored 1.43; V19 ran with high and scored 1.17.

Third, the FP8 KV cache may have introduced numerical instability that affected tool-call parsing. FP8 KV cache is a tradeoff: 2x cache size for ~1% accuracy loss. On ARC-AGI-3's strict win conditions, 1% may matter.

The V19 patch design was correct (all 9 fired), but the configuration was wrong. V24 later used 8 of these 9 patches (minus the reasoning_effort cap, which was unnecessary because the anim bundle already set it appropriately) and reached 2.56.
